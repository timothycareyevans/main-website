What this version is optimized for

What changed from the first prototype?

This one follows the visual rhythm of the main website project: brighter accent colors, pill-based navigation, a tighter editorial type mix, and flatter black section blocks.

What kind of pages does it fit best?

It is a strong fit for personal studio pages, tool directories, featured utilities, and conversion-focused project showcases.

Can it become a full product site?

Yes. The sections are already organized like a reusable landing system, so it can expand into a full multi-page site without redesigning from scratch.
